║  └─ Remove Selected     → Re-enable monitoring                      ║
║                                                                      ║
║  BUTTONS                                                             ║

║  └─ Shows: "X apps monitored (Y excluded)"                          ║
║                                                                      ║

║  └─ exclusions.json     → Excluded applications                     ║

║  COMMON TASKS                                                        ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║                                                                      ║
║  Hide an app:                                                        ║
║    1. Right-click the app                                           ║
║    2. Click "Exclude from monitoring"                               ║
║                                                                      ║
║  Show a hidden app:                                                  ║
║    1. Click ⚙ button                                                ║
║    2. Select the app                                                 ║
║    3. Click "Remove Selected"                                        ║
║    4. Click "Close"                                                  ║
║                                                                      ║
║  Manually exclude an app:                                            ║
║    1. Click ⚙ button                                                ║
║    2. Click "Add Executable..."                                      ║
║    3. Browse to the .exe file                                        ║
║    4. Click "Open"                                                   ║
║                                                                      ║
