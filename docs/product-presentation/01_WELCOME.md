## 🤔 What is Sound Manager?

Sound Manager is a **lightweight, elegant Windows application** that gives you **per-application volume control** right at your fingertips!

### 🎯 The Problem It Solves

Ever been in this situation?

- 🎮 **Gaming** while listening to **music**, but the game is too loud?
- 🎬 **Watching a video** while on a **voice call**, and can't hear your friend?
- 🔊 **Multiple apps** playing audio, and Windows mixer is buried deep in settings?

**Sound Manager fixes this!** 🎉

## 🎯 Core Concept

### Simple Philosophy:

```
One App = One Slider = Full Control
```

- **See** all your audio apps at a glance
- **Control** each one independently
- **Mute** any app with one click
- **Hide** apps you don't need to control

---

## 🚀 Ready to Start?

### Quick Actions:

1. **🎚️ Adjust Volume** → Drag any slider
2. **🔇 Mute App** → Click the speaker icon
3. **🙈 Hide App** → Right-click → Exclude
4. **⚙️ Manage Exclusions** → Click the cogwheel
