╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  QUICK START                                                         ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║                                                                      ║
║  1. Double-click SoundManager.exe                                    ║
║  2. Play audio in any app                                            ║
║  3. App appears automatically                                        ║
║  4. Drag slider to adjust volume                                     ║
║  5. Click 🔊/🔇 to mute/unmute                                      ║
║                                                                      ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  CONTROLS                                                            ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║                                                                      ║
║  VOLUME:        Drag slider left/right (0-100%)                      ║
║  MUTE:          Click 🔊 (green) → Muted 🔇 (red)                   ║
║  UNMUTE:        Click 🔇 (red) → Unmuted 🔊 (green)                 ║
║  HIDE APP:      Right-click → "Exclude from monitoring"             ║
║  SETTINGS:      Click ⚙ button (bottom-right)                       ║
║  HELP:          Click ? button (bottom-right)                        ║
║  MOVE:          Click and drag anywhere                              ║
║  RESIZE:        Drag left or right edge                              ║
║  MINIMIZE:      Click — button                                       ║
║  CLOSE:         Click ✕ button                                       ║
║                                                                      ║

║    → Right-click app                                                 ║
║    → Click "Exclude from monitoring"                                 ║

║  Show hidden apps:                                                   ║
║    → Click ⚙ button                                                 ║
║    → Select app                                                      ║

╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║  TROUBLESHOOTING                                                     ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║                                                                      ║
║  App doesn't appear:                                                 ║
║    → Make sure it's playing audio                                    ║
║    → Wait 5 seconds                                                  ║
║    → Check exclusions (⚙ button)                                    ║
║                                                                      ║
║  Windows security warning:                                           ║
║    → Click "More info"                                               ║
║    → Click "Run anyway"                                              ║
║    → (Normal for unsigned apps)                                      ║
║                                                                      ║
║  Slider doesn't move:                                                ║
║    → Release the slider                                              ║
║    → Wait 200ms for sync                                             ║
║                                                                      ║
║  Window off-screen:                                                  ║
║    → Delete config.json                                              ║
║    → Restart app                                                     ║
║                                                                      ║
